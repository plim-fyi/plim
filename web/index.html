Plim

Your notes, on every device.
Plain markdown, end-to-end encrypted, for you and the agents you trust.

  • Private by design. Your notes are encrypted on your device; the server keeps what it can't read. No account to create, no password to remember: a passkey or 12 words.
  • Everywhere you write. On your computer and your phone, offline too, in sync. A note's title and tags sit in its frontmatter, as other markdown tools keep them.
  • With your agents. Share a note with Claude or Codex and they read and edit it; plim does the same from your terminal.

                It sees what you share with agents
                It reads and edits the notes you share with agents, and keeps new things there when you ask. Every agent you connect sees them. To give it a note of yours, turn on Agents in the note’s Share.
                Nothing else
                Your other notes stay end-to-end encrypted, out of its reach, whatever their tags. It can’t delete notes.

                To answer it, Plim’s connector reads the notes you give it, and its answers come from the AI service. Disconnect any time in Settings.

                Agents now see what you share with them

                Tags no longer share notes: they only sort them. A note is shared with agents instead (Agents, in its Share), and every agent you connect sees the notes it’s on.

                  The ones you leave unticked stay yours: agents lose them, and they get new keys. Change any of them later in its Share.

                  Delete your account?

                  This deletes, from the server and for good:

                  • all your notes, and their history
                  • the notes you’ve shared: their links stop working for everyone
                  • the apps you’ve connected, like Claude: they lose access
                  • your passkeys’ sign-in, and every device’s session

                  It can’t be undone, and there’s no export yet: copy anything you want to keep first. Your passkeys stay in your password manager (iCloud Keychain, Google, 1Password…): delete them there. Server backups keep a copy until they’re replaced.

                      Profile

                      Shown next to your cursor in notes you share. Kept encrypted, like your notes. Until you set one, you’re a character from Middle-earth, the one shown in the box.

                      Editor

                      Edit and Preview side by side, as a third choice in a note's bar, on a wide screen. This device only.

                      Markdown

                      Notes are written in markdown: # Heading, **bold**, - [ ] task. A note opens in the editor, its markdown as it is; Preview shows it formatted, and a click on a word there edits at that word; Split, once turned on above, shows both side by side, scrolling together (Ctrl+/ goes round them, ⌘/ on a Mac). Switching keeps your place.

                      Shortcuts

                      Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic, Ctrl+E code, Ctrl+K a link (⌘ on a Mac). Ctrl+Enter ticks a task. A click in a link shows where it goes, to open or copy; Ctrl+click opens it. In a list, Enter starts the next item, Enter on an empty one ends the list, and Tab indents an item.

                      Appearance

                      Light or dark, or as your system has it. This device only.

                        Connections

                        Apps that read and edit the notes you share with agents.

                        Each one is separate (claude.ai and its apps, Claude Code on each computer): disconnect one and the others keep working.

                        Connect Claude
                        1. In Claude (claude.ai, or its apps), open Customize → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
                        2. Name it Plim, with the address
                        3. Choose Connect. Plim asks you to confirm here, then it appears on this page.
                        This Plim has no connector set up yet, so apps can’t connect over the web. Whoever runs it can add one (README: “Connecting Claude over the web”).
                        It sees the notes you share with agents, and nothing else. Claude Code connects the same way: claude mcp add --transport http plim and this address.
                        Terminal client
                        plim, for Linux and macOS: your notes in a terminal, and in scripts. It signs in with your recovery phrase, like another device, and sees all your notes.
                        Install it, or update it, with curl -fsSL https://plim.fyi/install.sh | sh, which downloads the latest release and checks its hash. Download →

                        What it did

                        Storage

                        Used
                        Your notes, their history and your shared notes, encrypted. Deleted notes count until they're deleted for good. Every version of a note is kept; if your space runs short, the oldest versions go first, so a note can always be saved.

                        Account

                        Account ID
                        Import notes
                        Markdown or text files, or a zip of them: an export from Plim, Obsidian or Bear. Each file becomes a note, encrypted on this device, and its folder a tag.
                        Export all notes
                        Every note as a Markdown file, in one zip, with its tags and dates. Made on this device. The files aren’t encrypted: keep them somewhere safe.
                        Delete account
                        Your notes, their history and the notes you’ve shared are deleted from the server, for good.
                        Needs a connection.
